Morning summary. The Herdr Boss service posts its own watch report. Add a short Boss summary in the Boss chat with `herdr-boss say` and in this pane.

1. List what merged and shipped, per project.
2. List the decisions that you made for the Owner.
3. List what is blocked and what needs the Owner.
4. List the retrospective findings and the model notes.
5. Run `herdr-boss watch stop` if the watch is still on.
6. Remove the temporary decisions that say the Boss acts for the Owner. Tell the orchestrators that the Owner is back.
